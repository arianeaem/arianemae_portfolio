Lustre & Glow

A modern, luxury-inspired salon website redesign

A modern, luxury-inspired website redesign for a Toronto-based hair and laser salon, focused on improving readability, strengthening brand perception, and increasing appointment bookings through a simplified user journey.

Background

Lustre & Glow is a Toronto-based hair and laser salon with premium services. I redesigned the website to improve readability, elevate brand perception, and create a faster, clearer path from browsing to booking.

My Role

End-to-end design | User research | Prototyping | Usability testing

Timeline

Sept 2024 - Nov 2024 (12 weeks)

Deliverables

Wireframes | Prototypes | Design System | Usability Report

The Challenge

To transform a dense, outdated website into a clean, editorial experience that highlights premium services, improves readability, and drives bookings through a refined, user-first design approach.

How I approached the solution

Strategy & UX Approach

The goal was to design a site that reflects luxury while maintaining ease of use. The experience needed to orient users quickly, reduce cognitive load, present services clearly, and guide users toward booking without overwhelming them.

Key UX Principles

Clarity over complexity Design for fast decisions Scan, don't overwhelm Conversion-driven layout

What I designed

Key Design Decisions

The redesign focused on reducing cognitive overload while aligning the visual experience with the salon's premium positioning. Every decision prioritized clarity, speed, and conversion.

Clear 3-Second Hero

Redesigned the hero section to instantly communicate services and value within seconds, helping users understand what the salon offers without scrolling.

Scannable Pricing Structure

Transformed dense pricing tables into a cleaner, more digestible format, allowing users to quickly find services and make booking decisions with confidence.

Shortened, SEO-Friendly Content

Condensed long text sections into concise, skimmable content while retaining key keywords, improving both user experience and search visibility.

Self-Reflection

This project reinforced how critical first impressions are, especially in service-based businesses where users decide quickly whether to stay or leave.

I learned that simplifying content doesn't mean losing value; it often strengthens both clarity and conversion when done strategically.

It also highlighted the importance of aligning visual design with brand perception - luxury services require an equally refined digital experience.
